Common questions

  • My progress/stats disappeared after reinstalling. Lucidmi syncs your profile and game history via your iCloud account. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID and that iCloud is enabled for Lucidmi in your device's Settings.
  • How do I manage or cancel Lucidmi's Ascent+? Open Lucidmi, go to Settings, and tap "Manage Subscription" — this opens Apple's own subscription management, where you can change plans or cancel at any time.
  • How do I restore a purchase on a new device? In Lucidmi's Settings, tap "Restore Purchases."
