Lucidmi Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Lucidmi ("the app") is a memory-training game published by myelininitiatives. This policy explains what information Lucidmi collects, how it's used, and your choices.

What Lucidmi collects

Everything Lucidmi collects is information you enter directly while using the app, in order to run the app's own features:

  • Profile information — the display name, age, and gender you choose to enter (gender includes a "prefer not to say" option and is never required).
  • Profile photo — if you choose to set one via your device's camera, instead of one of the app's built-in emoji avatars.
  • Gameplay data — your game sessions, scores, accuracy, rank/level progress, streaks, and achievement unlocks, generated as you play.
  • Preferences — settings you choose, such as daily reminder times and app theme.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active Lucidmi's Ascent+ subscription, so premium features unlock correctly.

How your data is stored

Lucidmi stores this information using Apple's SwiftData and iCloud (CloudKit) frameworks, synced to your own private iCloud account — the same way Apple's own apps (Notes, Reminders, Photos) sync your data across your devices. Lucidmi does not operate its own servers, and the app's developer does not have access to your data — it lives in your personal iCloud account, under your control.

What Lucidmi does not do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks or advertising SDKs are included in the app.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking services.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • Lucidmi does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Camera access

If you grant camera access, it's used solely to let you take a profile photo within the app. That photo is stored the same way as the rest of your profile data — synced to your own iCloud account, not uploaded to any server Lucidmi's developer operates.

Subscriptions and payment

Lucidmi's Ascent+ subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Payment details (card numbers, billing information) are processed by Apple and are never seen by Lucidmi or its developer.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, these are scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's local notification system. No data about your reminder activity leaves your device.

Children's privacy

Lucidmi is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your data, your control

Because your data lives in your own iCloud account, deleting the app or your Lucidmi data from your device (or from iCloud in your device's Settings) removes it. If you have questions about your data or would like help removing it, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised, and material changes will be reflected here.
